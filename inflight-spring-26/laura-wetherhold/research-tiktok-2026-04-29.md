## Summary

ToneAdapt's approach to marketing the Protocol Grip Trainer via TikTok micro-influencers represents a replicable model for founder-led startups. This research brief analyzes the strategy and provides actionable recommendations.

## The ToneAdapt Model

### What They Did
- **Self-referential problem identification:** "I have this problem, I'll create a solution"
- **Micro-influencer targeting:** 1k-10k follower accounts in guitar/learning niche
- **TikTok-first:** Leveraged algorithm's preference for niche content
- **Community validation:** Found other people like them (anyone who plays guitar)

### Why It Worked
1. **Authenticity:** Real person with real problem
2. **Algorithm alignment:** TikTok rewards niche, consistent content
3. **Influencer accessibility:** Micro-influencers more approachable and affordable
4. **Community resonance:** Self-selection into the problem space

## Comparative Analysis: TikTok vs Reddit

| Factor | TikTok | Reddit |
|--------|--------|--------|
| **Discovery** | Algorithm-driven | Community/search-driven |
| **Engagement** | High for short-form | High for deep discussion |
| **Founder Fit** | Visual/demo products | Technical/community products |
| **Cost** | Free (organic) | Free (organic) |
| **Approach** | Influencer partnerships | Direct community participation |

**Key Insight:** TikTok for discovery, Reddit for validation. Not either/or but sequence.

## Actionable Recommendations

### For Your Startup (LaunchMate)

1. **Identify your "guitar players"**
   - Who has the problem you're solving?
   - Where do they hang out online?
   - What micro-influencers do they follow?

2. **TikTok Strategy**
   - Create founder-led content ("day in the life of building LaunchMate")
   - Identify 20-30 micro-influencers in founder/startup space
   - Reach out with specific collaboration proposals (not generic)

3. **Reddit Strategy**
   - r/startups, r/SaaS, r/Entrepreneur for validation
   - Share learnings, not pitches
   - Build credibility before mentioning product

### For Other Founders (if advising)

- **Physical products:** TikTok for demos, unboxing, ASMR
- **Software:** Reddit for technical validation, TikTok for "how I built this"
- **Services:** LinkedIn + TikTok hybrid (credibility + discovery)
