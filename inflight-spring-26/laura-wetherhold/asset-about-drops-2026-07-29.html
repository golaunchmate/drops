A Drop About Drops

The LaunchMate drop system, explained by a drop

How a drop flows

🎙️Transcript AgentListens, extracts insights
↓
📮Drop AgentDispatches to the right founder
↓
🤖Founder AgentOwns it - creates the asset
↓
🐙GitHub PagesStores it, makes it live
↓
✨UserGets the drop. Done.

Four types of drops

Briefings

Session summaries - key decisions, commitments, open questions from transcripts.

Reminders

Commitment nudges - "you said you would do X by Tuesday. It is Tuesday."

Assets

Generated content - guides, one-pagers, visual resources, or this very page.

Research

Deep dives - market analysis, competitor intel, strategy explorations.

URL Pattern

https://golaunchmate.github.io/drops/{cohort}/{user}/{drop-type}-{date}.{ext}

Naming conventions

  • 📁Cohort folders: inflight-spring-26/, bootcamp-spring-26/, community/
  • 👤User folders: laura-wetherhold/, michael-hayes/, craig-lauer/
  • 🏷️Drop-type prefixes: briefing-, reminder-, asset-, research-
  • 📅Date suffix: YYYY-MM-DD - always the session date, not delivery date
  • 📄Extensions: .html for visual drops, .md for text, .json for structured data
