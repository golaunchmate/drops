The Case Study

Jay's Muse case study — the casino inspector — is the clearest signal we have for how idea-less domain experts actually produce ideas worth building on. The inspector didn't start with a startup idea. He started with years of embodied expertise and friction he'd normalized. Muse's job was to surface the "why now" idea hiding inside that experience.

Capture Patterns Worth Productizing

PATTERN P1
On-site, in-flow capture
The inspector's insights came out where he worked, not in a scheduled brainstorm. Capture has to meet the expert inside their daily routine.
PATTERN P2
Expertise-first, idea-second
The flow starts from what the person knows and lives, not from a blank "what's your idea?" prompt. Authenticity as the input, ideas as the output.
PATTERN P3
"Why now" as the forcing function
Raw observations get sharpened into ideas by asking what changed in the world that makes this newly possible or newly urgent.
