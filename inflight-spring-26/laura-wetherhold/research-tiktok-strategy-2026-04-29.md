## Key Finding

TikTok has emerged as the dominant platform for micro-influencer marketing, while Reddit remains underutilized for direct outreach in the founder/startup space.

## Data Points

### TikTok
- **High engagement** for "day in the life" content from founders
- **Discovery algorithm** favors niche content (guitar learning, startup journeys)
- **Micro-influencer strategy** pioneered by ToneAdapt: target 1k-10k followers in specific niches
- **Cost-effective** compared to Instagram or YouTube

### Reddit
- **Community-focused** but high skepticism toward promotion
- **Subreddit discovery** requires deep knowledge of communities
- **Success stories** exist but are more educational than promotional
- **Recommendation:** Use for validation and feedback, not primary marketing

## Strategic Recommendations

### For Software Products (like Protocol Grip Trainer)
1. **Primary:** TikTok micro-influencer outreach
2. **Secondary:** Reddit for authentic community engagement
3. **Avoid:** Reddit direct promotion (will backfire)

### For Content Creators
1. **TikTok** for discovery and algorithmic reach
2. **Instagram** for deeper engagement
3. **Reddit** for community credibility
